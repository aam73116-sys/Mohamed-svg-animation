Reflection: The hardest animation for me was the self-drawing line, because I had to understand how stroke-dasharray and stroke-dashoffset work together. I looked it up and learned that stroke-dasharray sets the length of the dash, and when it equals the whole line length, offsetting it hides the line, then animating the offset to zero makes it look like the line is being drawn. Outside of class I have seen these patterns in real life: the hover transform on buttons and icons on almost every website, the spinning loader whenever a page or app is loading, the self-drawing line in logo intro animations on brand websites, and the fade-up entrance as sections appear while scrolling down landing pages. Building all four myself helped me understand the small details behind animations I see every day.
